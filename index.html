How I work

A clear process, flexible by design

  1. 01

    Discover

    Gather requirements with stakeholders, run user research and map journeys to understand the real problem.

  2. 02

    Define

    Shape personas, information architecture and user flows that align user needs with business goals.

  3. 03

    Design

    Move from wireframes to high-fidelity UI and interactive prototypes, using AI to explore more directions, faster.

  4. 04

    Deliver & iterate

    Test with users, hand off with tokens and specs, and keep improving based on real insights.
