What I do

From the first research session to developer handoff, I take products end to end and keep them consistent as they grow.

Product & UI/UX design

Web and mobile products designed from wireframes to high-fidelity UI, with interaction design and responsive layouts that put the user first.

Design systems

Reusable components, design tokens and UI patterns that keep brands consistent across platforms and make design-to-dev collaboration efficient.

UX research & usability testing

User research, personas, journey mapping and information architecture, then usability testing to iterate on real insights.

AI-assisted design

Claude AI, Figma AI and Cursor built into my workflow to explore more directions, prototype faster and design AI-powered product experiences.

Prototyping & developer handoff

Interactive prototypes in Figma and Framer, plus clear specs, tokens and documentation so engineers can build exactly what was designed.
