Overview

Learn, compete and grow through an intuitive and enjoyable experience.

Lumora is a mobile-first trivia platform designed to transform learning into an engaging and rewarding experience.

This end-to-end UX case study covers the complete product design journey, including user research, information architecture, branding, design systems, wireframes, and high-fidelity mobile interfaces.
