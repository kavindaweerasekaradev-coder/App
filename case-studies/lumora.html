01 · Overview

Transforming learning into an engaging trivia experience.

Lumora is a mobile-first trivia platform designed to make learning more engaging, interactive, and rewarding. By combining knowledge-based challenges with gamification mechanics, users can test their skills, compete with others, earn achievements, and track their growth through a seamless mobile experience.

The idea

The goal was to create an experience that feels less like studying and more like playing, encouraging users to return daily and continuously expand their knowledge.

03 · The challenge

Many learning and trivia applications struggle to keep users engaged after the first few interactions.

As a result, users often abandon learning platforms before developing long-term habits.

Common challenges included

  • Low retention rates
  • Repetitive learning experiences
  • Lack of meaningful rewards
  • Weak competitive elements
  • Limited personalization
  • Poor visibility of progress

04 · The goal

The primary objective was to design a platform that motivates users to learn consistently through engagement, competition, and rewards.

Objectives

  • Increase user engagement
  • Encourage daily participation
  • Create meaningful progression
  • Improve quiz completion rates
  • Foster healthy competition
  • Build long-term learning habits

05 · Research & discovery

Research was conducted by analyzing existing trivia platforms, studying user behavior, and identifying factors that contribute to user motivation.

  1. 01

    Users Prefer Short Sessions

    Users were more likely to engage with quizzes that could be completed within a few minutes.

  2. 02

    Progress Drives Motivation

    Visible achievements and progress indicators encouraged continued participation.

  3. 03

    Competition Increases Retention

    Leaderboards and rankings created a sense of achievement and encouraged repeat visits.

  4. 04

    Rewards Build Habits

    Badges, points, streaks, and achievements increased user motivation over time.

06 · Problem & solution

How might we create a learning platform that transforms knowledge acquisition into a rewarding and competitive experience while maintaining simplicity and accessibility?

Lumora combines personalized learning paths, engaging trivia challenges, competitive rankings, and achievement systems into a unified mobile experience.

The platform allows users to

  • Discover quizzes based on interests
  • Participate in daily challenges
  • Earn points and achievements
  • Compete on leaderboards
  • Track learning progress

09 · User personas

Two learners, two ways to play.

E

Emily Carter, the Competitive Learner

Age 23 · Austin, Texas, USA · University Student

Emily enjoys learning through interactive experiences during her commute and lunch breaks. She is highly motivated by achievements, streaks, and friendly competition, making trivia apps part of her daily routine.

Goals

  • Learn new topics every day
  • Improve general knowledge
  • Maintain daily learning streaks
  • Compete on leaderboards

Needs

  • Achievement system
  • Fast and intuitive experience
  • Progress dashboard

Frustrations

  • Repetitive quizzes
  • Lack of motivation
  • Difficult quiz navigation
  • No visible progress tracking
D

David Fernando, the Casual Professional

Age 31 · Seattle, USA · Software Engineer

David enjoys spending a few minutes each day learning something new during breaks. He values simplicity and prefers quick quiz sessions that fit into his busy schedule.

Goals

  • Learn in short sessions
  • Discover interesting topics
  • Track learning progress
  • Stay mentally active

Needs

  • Quick access to quizzes
  • Personalized content
  • Simple navigation
  • Performance insights

Frustrations

  • Long onboarding
  • Complex interfaces
  • Time-consuming quizzes
  • Poor recommendations

15 · Wallet

The Wallet screen centralizes users' rewards, balances, and transaction history into a single, easy-to-navigate interface.

By prioritizing clarity and transparency, users can quickly monitor their earnings, redeem rewards, and stay motivated through visible progress.

Highlights

  • Transparent reward tracking

16 · Branding

A strong product deserves a memorable identity.

The Lumora brand was designed to be flexible across both digital and physical mediums, creating a consistent experience wherever users interact with the brand.

17 · Outcome

The final product delivers a seamless learning experience where users can discover quizzes, test their knowledge, compete with others, and celebrate achievements through a visually engaging interface.

The design balances simplicity with motivation, ensuring users remain focused while enjoying every interaction.

User impact

  • Increased engagement through gamification
  • Clear visibility of learning progress
  • Improved quiz completion rates
  • Stronger motivation through rewards
  • More enjoyable learning experience

Business impact

  • Higher daily active users
  • Improved retention
  • Longer average session duration
  • Increased challenge participation
  • Scalable product foundation

18 · Reflection

Designing Lumora reinforced the importance of creating products that motivate users beyond functionality.

Rather than treating quizzes as isolated tasks, the experience was designed as a continuous journey where every interaction contributes to personal growth.

Curious minds never stop learning.

The combination of personalization, progression, and competition transforms learning into a habit users genuinely enjoy.
