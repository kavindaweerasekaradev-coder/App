Overview

Discover, save, plan and visualise a whole trip in one seamless experience.

Wanderly is a modern travel discovery and planning platform designed to make trip planning simple, inspiring, and effortless.

The project explores how users can discover destinations, save travel ideas, build personalised itineraries, and visualise their journeys in one seamless experience.
