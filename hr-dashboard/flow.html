Log in

login.html
  1. Enter work email and password
  2. Wrong details show an error and stay here
  3. Correct details open the dashboard
  4. Forgot password: enter email, then “Link sent”, then back to log in
Leads to DashboardSign upReset password

Sign up

signup.html
  1. Create account: name, email, password, terms
  2. Verify email with a 6-digit code
  3. Company name, team size and industry
  4. Invite teammates, or skip
  5. “All set” summary, then the dashboard
Leads to DashboardLog in

Dashboard

index.html
  1. Greets you by name and time of day
  2. Headcount, attendance chart, payroll and today’s attendance
  3. Approve or decline leave requests
  4. Click a name in the team table to open their profile
Leads to EmployeesRecruitmentAdd employeeA profileSign out

Employees

employees.html
  1. Search, filter by status or department, sort, export
  2. Open a profile: change status, see manager and time off
  3. Remove someone, with a confirmation and undo
  4. Add employee in 3 steps: personal, job, review
Leads to DashboardSign out

Recruitment

recruitment.html
  1. Pick a role to filter the pipeline, or post a new job in 3 steps
  2. Drag candidates between Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer and Hired
  3. Open a candidate to rate them, add notes, book an interview or reject
  4. Mark as hired, then create their employee profile with details filled in
